I'm Cliff Matthews, aka [Lazy Young
deadhead](https://ctm.github.io/docs/yld/life/too-public.html). I'm
overwhelmed, but not burnt out. I recently completed
the [Hardrock 100](https://hardrock100.com/) and "attended"<sup class="footnote-reference"><a
href="#1">1</a></sup> [BARGE](https://www.barge.org/). Those vacations recharged
my batteries thoroughly.

Although this is the first post in this blog, it's not a "start here"
entry. Mb2 is a project I've been working on for over seven years<sup
class="footnote-reference"><a href="#2">2</a></sup>.

## "Coming Soon"

Craftpoker.com has been around for years. We've been playing a
tournament a day since _before_ May 1st, 2020<sup
class="footnote-reference"><a href="#3">3</a></sup>. Although we've
been play-testing in the open, we haven't tried to catch people's
attention. I will do so soon, but there's infrastructure
that needs to be in place, first.  This blog is one piece of that
infrastructure, and for now it's a mere placeholder.

## No Kickstarter, yet

The two primary stakeholders of Craft Poker Co. are players and
investors. 

After we get rid of the [intimidating
warning](https://ctm.github.io/docs/players_manual/native-apps.html#microsoft-defender-will-object-to-the-windows-installer)
that the Native Windows installer produces and after we get a [nicer
UI](https://ctm.github.io/docs/players_manual/coming-soon.html#user-interface),
we plan to do a Kickstarter, raising money to build out the rest of
the
[near-term features](https://ctm.github.io/docs/players_manual/coming-soon.html).

## Developers

Mb2 is built on [Rust](https://rust-lang.org/), the
programming language, [Yew](https://yew.rs/), the Rust front-end framework and
[Tauri](https://v2.tauri.app/), the cross-platform wrapper.
Additionally, we use the [DecentSecret Tauri
plugin](https://github.com/decentpaste/decentpaste/tree/dev/decentpaste-app/tauri-plugin-decentsecret)
and our own [css-asset-pack](https://github.com/ctm/css-asset-pack)
and [serde_de_chain](https://github.com/ctm/serde_de_chain). Although
the vast majority of Mb2's source is closed, both css-asset-pack and
serde_de_chain are open source.

I anticipate writing blog posts for developers<sup
class="footnote-reference"><a href="#4">4</a></sup>.
